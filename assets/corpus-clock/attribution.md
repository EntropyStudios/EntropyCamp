# Corpus Clock asset attribution

The layered Chronophage assets in this directory are derivatives of
[`Chronophage pol.jpg`](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Chronophage_pol.jpg),
photographed by Wikimedia Commons user **Rror** on 4 October 2008.

- Source license: [CC BY-SA 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/)
- Changes: background removal, layer separation, crop/registration, frontal dial
  rectification, colour grading, resizing and WebP conversion.
- The derived Chronophage image layers remain available under CC BY-SA 3.0.

The fixed dial texture is a frontal, circular rectification of the same CC
BY-SA photograph. The separately animated escapement wheel, apertures and
mechanical geometry were informed by Corpus Clock patent
[`US8218400B2`](https://patents.google.com/patent/US8218400B2/en).
